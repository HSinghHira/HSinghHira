About Me

Chandigarh kid who chose
HTML over FIFA

Born in Chandigarh, India 🇮🇳. Moved to Aotearoa New Zealand in my early 20s — packed my whole life into two suitcases and called it a main character move.

At 15, while most friends were yelling into headsets, I was building websites and selling templates. By 16, a tech blog running AdSense was making real money — all self-taught through YouTube, forums, and a lot of trial and error. No school. No courses. Just curiosity carrying heavy.

Coding is my therapy. Just me, the screen, late-night silence, and a random idea turning into something real. I keep it as my playground — the moment it feels forced, the magic fades. So I never let it become a job.

These days I'm a happily married dad living in NZ, still chasing ideas at odd hours, still building random tools, still curious, still vibing. 🥂
