When you move to New Zealand, everyone tells you about the scenery. The mountains, the beaches, the Lord of the Rings stuff. 

Nobody tells you about the *real* stuff.

## 1. The Houses are Cold
You'd think a developed country would understand insulation. You would be wrong. It's often colder inside the house than it is outside.

## 2. Four Seasons in One Day
You leave the house in a t-shirt because it's sunny. By lunchtime, it's pouring rain. By 3 PM, you're wearing a jumper. By 5 PM, it's sunny again. Always bring layers.

## 3. The Coffee is Actually Incredible
I didn't know what good coffee was until I moved here. A flat white in Auckland ruins you for coffee anywhere else in the world.
