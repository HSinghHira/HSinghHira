Before having a kid, my default speed was *fast*. Fast walking, fast eating, fast coding. Get things done, move to the next thing.

Then my kid arrived.

Try rushing a toddler who has just discovered a particularly interesting leaf on the sidewalk. You can't. 

They don't care about your schedule. They don't care that you're running 5 minutes late. That leaf is the most important thing in the world right now.

At first, it frustrated me. Then, I realized something: I hadn't looked at a leaf in probably 20 years. 

They force you to slow down. To operate on their frequency. It's exhausting, but honestly, it's exactly what I needed.
