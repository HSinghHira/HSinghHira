This is a true story about trying to use public transport in Auckland. 

I did everything right. I tagged on. I tagged off. I had $20 sitting on my HOP card. But somehow, somewhere in the mysterious void of Auckland Transport's backend systems, I was deemed a fare evader.

A week later, an envelope arrived with a $70 fine. 

## The Appeals Process

Calling them is an exercise in patience. You get passed between departments until you speak to someone who agrees it makes no sense, but tells you they can't cancel it over the phone.

So I wrote an email. Attached screenshots of my transit history showing the tag on and off. Three weeks later, they replied. 

*“After reviewing your case, we have decided to waive the fine on this occasion as a gesture of goodwill.”*

Goodwill? You literally made a mistake. But I took the win. Welcome to NZ.
