This was where everything started. I was 15, had just discovered that you could build things for the internet, and I wanted to show the world what I could do.

## The Beginning

I learned entirely from YouTube tutorials and late-night forum dives. No formal training, no bootcamp — just a browser, a text editor, and a burning curiosity. I still remember the first time a layout I built actually looked the way I imagined it. That feeling was everything.

## What I built

A simple but clean personal portfolio showcasing my skills and projects. It wasn't fancy — but it was mine. Hand-coded from scratch, every line of HTML and CSS written by a teenager who had no idea this would become a lifelong craft.

## What it taught me

That shipping is better than perfect. That real projects teach you more than any tutorial. And that building something on the internet is one of the most powerful things a person can do.
